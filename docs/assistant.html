Grounded assistant

Ask a safety question. The assistant retrieves from a fixed, governed corpus, quotes the best passage with its clause, or abstains when the evidence is weak.

No language model is involved. Retrieval is BM25+ running in your browser. The answer is the top passage quoted verbatim (extractive), so it cannot say anything the corpus does not. This is a demonstration of the pattern, not safety advice; check the cited regulation before acting. Corpus: verified passages from the ehs-ai-grounding-eval benchmark.
