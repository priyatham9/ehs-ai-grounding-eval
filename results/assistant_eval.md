# Grounded assistant: benchmark evaluation

**No language model was used.** The answer composer is `MockModel`, which is extractive: it quotes the top retrieved corpus passage verbatim and cites its clause. Retrieval is the repository's BM25+ index. Everything below is deterministic and regenerated by `python3 scripts/run_assistant_eval.py`.

Corpus digest `a9fd4f4c2222dfb9`; 68 items (63 factual, 5 category-error). Abstain rule: answer only if the top passage's IDF-weighted query-token coverage is at least the threshold (default 0.30; see `docs/assistant.md`).

Caveat: as in the repository's retrieval baselines, the governing passage of every question is in the index (the question text is not removed from it), so retrieval is tested for selecting that passage, not for finding evidence in an unseen corpus. Citation precision is the share of answered factual items whose cited clause grades `correct_clause` under the repository's citation scorer.
